Deck & Collect help

Deck & Collect keeps the Pokémon cards you own, the decks you build and the cards you still want, on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. These pages are for someone who has just installed it.

The pages are in the order a new person needs them. If you only read one, read the first.

  1. Get startedOpen the app for the first time, add your first sets, and find your way around.
  2. Find cardsBrowse sets, search by name or card text, and open a card.
  3. Record the cards you ownCount each copy by printing, type, language, condition and grade.
  4. Scan cards with the cameraAdd cards from your iPhone or iPad without typing.
  5. Build a deck and check itBuild or import a deck, see what you are missing, and check the rules.
  6. Wishlists and missing cardsKeep wishlists, and let the app list what your decks still need.
  7. Print a wishlist or a deck listMake a PDF checklist for the shop, or a deck list for a tournament.
  8. Match logsPaste a match log from Pokémon TCG Live and read the game back.
  9. Your devices and your backupsWhat syncs between iPhone, iPad and Mac, and how to back up on a Mac.
  10. Troubleshooting and questionsAnswers to the problems people meet first.

Good to know before you start

  • The app starts empty. You choose which sets to add, and it downloads them. There is no card list to import and no spreadsheet import or export in this version: you add cards one at a time, or with the camera on iPhone and iPad.
  • No account, no ads, and nothing is collected about you. Your collection syncs between your own devices through your own iCloud.
  • The card data and pictures come from TCGdex, a free public card database.
  • Every picture on these pages uses an invented set called Verdigris Hollow, so none of the cards shown are real.
