Deck & Collect Support Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Deck & Collect collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you put in the app stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What the app stores

Everything you record is kept on your device:

  • your collection, your decks and your wishlists
  • the match logs you import
  • the friends you lend cards to, and what they borrowed
  • your player details for a printed tournament deck list: name, Player ID and date of birth
  • your settings

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs this between your own devices through your private iCloud account. Apple stores it under your Apple Account and only you can read it. The developer cannot see it. Apple explains how it handles iCloud data in its own privacy policy.

You can remove your data at any time. Delete it in the app, or remove the app's data under iCloud in your device's settings.

What the app downloads

The app contacts two outside services, and only when you use the feature that needs them. Neither request says anything about you or your collection. Like any website you visit, those services see your IP address.

ServiceWhenWhat is downloaded
TCGdex (tcgdex.net)When you build a card pack in PacksCard data and card pictures for the sets you chose
Cardmarket (cardmarket.com)Only if you turn on Settings ▸ Cardmarket, which is off to begin withCardmarket's public price list

Links to Cardmarket open in your browser. From there on, Cardmarket's own privacy policy applies.

A pack file you import yourself is read from where you saved it. Nothing is uploaded.

The camera

The card scanner on iPhone and iPad is optional and off to begin with. It uses the camera only while the scanner is open. Each picture is compared with the cards in your installed packs, on your device. Pictures are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

The AI connection on Mac

On a Mac you can turn on a connection that lets an AI assistant you run yourself read and change your collection and decks. It is off to begin with, works only on that Mac, and is protected by a secret token. The app sends nothing through it by itself.

Children

Deck & Collect is not aimed at children, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
