Deck & Collect Help Support Privacy policy

Support

Stuck, or found something that looks wrong? Start with the questions below. If your answer is not here, write to us.

Contact

Write to deckandcollect@icloud.com. Say which device you use and which version of the app you have. You find the version under Settings ▸ General ▸ About.

The app is empty when I open it

Deck & Collect comes without cards. You choose which sets to add. On the Cards screen you have two ways to begin:

  • Try with Demo Data fills the app with an invented set, a deck, a small collection and a match log, so you can look around. Remove it again under Settings ▸ General ▸ Demo data.
  • Build a Pack takes you to Packs. Pick the sets you want, and the app downloads their cards and pictures.

My collection is missing on my other device

Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account. Syncing can take a few minutes. Settings ▸ General shows whether iCloud sync is working.

Card packs are not synced. Build or import them on each device.

Cards or sets look wrong or out of date

Open Settings ▸ Troubleshooting and choose Reset Card Database…. The app rebuilds its card list from your packs. Your collection, decks and match logs are not touched.

The scanner does not recognise my card

The scanner only knows cards from the packs you have installed, so add the card's set first. Then:

  • lay one card on a plain, darker table
  • use even light, without glare on the card
  • move closer so the card fills more of the picture

The scanner is on iPhone and iPad. Turn it on under Settings ▸ General ▸ Card scanner.

Where do the prices come from

The remaining cost of a wishlist or a deck comes from Cardmarket's public price list. Turn it on under Settings ▸ Cardmarket. It is an estimate, not the price you will pay.
