Deck & Collect Help Support Privacy policy

Support

Stuck, or found something that looks wrong? Start with the questions below. If your answer is not here, write to us.

Contact

Write to deckandcollect@icloud.com. Say which device you use and which version of the app you have. You find the version under Settings ▸ General ▸ About.

The app is empty when I open it

Deck & Collect comes without cards. You choose which sets to add.

  • Add Sets, on the empty Cards screen, takes you to Manage Sets. Pick the sets you want, and the app downloads their cards and pictures.
  • To look around first, Settings ▸ General ▸ Demo data ▸ Try with Demo Data fills the app with an invented set, a deck, a small collection and a match log. Remove it again in the same place.

My collection is missing on my other device

Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account. Syncing can take a few minutes. Settings ▸ General ▸ iCloud Sync shows whether it is working.

The card data of your sets does not go through iCloud. Each device downloads it from TCGdex itself: on Wi-Fi, the sets you added on another device download by themselves. Turn that off under Settings ▸ General ▸ Sets ▸ Download sets from my other devices.

Cards or sets look wrong or out of date

Open Settings ▸ Troubleshooting and choose Reset Card Database…. The app rebuilds its card list from your sets. Your collection, decks and match logs are not touched.

The scanner does not recognise my card

The scanner only knows cards from the sets you added, so add the card's set first in Manage Sets. Then:

  • lay one card on a plain, darker table
  • use even light, without glare on the card
  • move closer so the card fills more of the picture

The scanner is on iPhone and iPad. Turn it on under Settings ▸ General ▸ Card scanner.
