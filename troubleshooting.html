Troubleshooting and questions

If your problem is not here, write to deckandcollect@icloud.com. Say which device you use and the version of the app, which you find under Settings ▸ General ▸ About.

The app is empty

That is normal. Add the sets you own: Get started. To look around first, use Settings ▸ General ▸ Demo data.

The list of sets does not load

It says Can't load the list of sets. Check your internet connection, then tap Try Again.

One set failed to download

Its row says why, in red. Tap Try Again on that row. The other sets are not affected. Sometimes the card database is missing part of a set. Try again later.

A card has no picture, or a soft picture

  • A soft picture is the small one. The sharp picture downloads when you open the card large and you are online.
  • With no connection it says Full size needs an internet connection. It loads by itself when you are back online.
  • With Low Data Mode on, tap Load Full Size under the picture.
  • To have sharp pictures for a whole set without internet, tap Download Full-Size Pictures on its row in Manage Sets.
  • A few cards have no picture in the card database yet. They show a grey placeholder.

A card says "Set not added"

Its set is not on this device. Add it in Manage Sets.

My collection is missing on my other device

Both devices need the same Apple Account and iCloud turned on. Wait a few minutes and check Settings ▸ General ▸ iCloud Sync. The sets themselves are not synced, but the other device downloads them when you open the app on Wi-Fi. See Your devices and your backups.

My deck says it is missing cards I own

Your copies belong to one real deck at a time. See Build a deck and check it, and use Virtual for decks that are only ideas.

Importing a deck says "Not Matched"

The set of that card is not added yet. Add it in Manage Sets and paste the list again.

The scanner does not recognise my card

See Scan cards with the camera: add the card's set first, use a plain darker table, even light and move closer.

Cards or sets look wrong or out of date

Open Settings ▸ Troubleshooting and choose Reset Card Database…. The app rebuilds its card list from your sets. Your collection, decks and match logs are not touched.

Can I import or export a spreadsheet?

Not in this version. You add cards by hand or with the scanner. You can copy and paste deck lists to and from Pokémon TCG Live, and print wishlists and deck lists as PDFs. On a Mac, an optional AI assistant connection can read your collection (see Your devices and your backups).

What data does the app collect?

None. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
