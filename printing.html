Print a wishlist or a deck list

The app makes two PDFs: a wishlist you can tick off in a shop, and a deck list for a tournament judge. Both are A4. On iPhone and iPad the PDF is handed to the Files sheet, where you save it or print it. On a Mac a save panel asks where to put it.

Print a wishlist

  1. Open Wishlists and choose a list, From Decks or All Wishes.
  2. Tap the print button in the toolbar (Export PDF…).
  3. Save the PDF, or open it and print it.

The PDF is grouped by set. Each card shows how many you want, with one box to tick for each copy.

Print a tournament deck list

This is a deck list in the layout of the Play! Pokémon form: your name and player ID, your age division, the format, and the Pokémon, Trainer and Energy cards with quantity, set code and collector number.

Fill in your details once

  1. Open Settings ▸ Player.
  2. Enter your Player Name and Player ID.
  3. If you want your date of birth printed, keep Use my date of birth on and set Date of Birth. The app suggests an Age Division. You can pick Junior, Senior or Masters yourself.
  4. Under Deck list, choose the Date format and whether to print today's date.

Make the PDF

  1. Open the deck and tap Deck List in its toolbar. On iPhone it may be inside the ⋯ menu.
  2. If something needs your attention, an alert says so first. Blank player details are the usual reason. Choose Fill In Details…, or Save Anyway.
  3. Save the PDF, then print it.
The app does not check that the deck is legal before it makes the list, and the age division is the app's own calculation from your birth year. Check both against the rules for the season you are playing. The form's other fields, such as the event, are for you to fill in by hand.
